The house

Founded 2016
Eight formulations
One laboratory
One stretch of coast

Most skincare is assembled from ingredients bought by the kilo from the same six suppliers. We built the house the other way round — starting with a stretch of coast, a licence to harvest it, and a question about what survives in cold water.

Everything below the tide line is under pressure. Cold, salt, shifting light, and a barrier to hold against all of it. The compounds those organisms make in order to cope are the ones we are interested in, because skin has the same job and solves it the same way.

So we file every formula by the depth its principal active was drawn from. It isn't a device. It's the batch record, and it is printed on the box.

The depth index

Eight formulations, ordered by the depth they are drawn from.

Seawater takes the light out of itself as it goes down. Below roughly fifty metres less than one percent of the surface reaches you, and the things still growing there have concentrated everything they have. This page descends at the same rate.

Method

The four-minute evening.

These are elapsed times, not durations. The waiting is the part people skip, and it is the part that decides how much of a serum ever gets in. Applied to a dry face, most of it does about half of what it can.

0:00

Surface Cleansing Milk

Thirty seconds on dry skin, then add water to emulsify and rinse. Leave the face damp — the next step depends on it.

0:45

Saline Essence

Two pumps pressed in with flat palms. No cotton pad. You are laying down a wetting layer, not removing anything.

1:15

Tidal Repair Serum

Three drops while the essence is still tacky. Then stop and wait — a full minute, until the tack has gone.

2:30

Kelp Forest Cream

A pea, warmed between the fingers first. Press it over the top. In hard winter, a second pass on the cheeks.

What the panel measured.

Sixty-two participants, twelve weeks, nightly use of the Tidal Repair Serum with the Kelp Forest Cream. Two of the three figures below are self-reported; the third is instrumental.

82%

reported a visible evening of tone at eight weeks

91%

barrier function intact after twelve weeks of nightly use

−34%

mean change in water loss, measured week 0 to week 8

Independent panel, Rennes, spring 2025. Transepidermal water loss by open-chamber evaporimetry. This is a demonstration site: every figure, product and person on it is invented.
